About the Founder
Kat Moore
Principal Stager & Creative Director

My mission is to help people live a more intentional, productive life and surround themselves with the things they love. I believe that strategic organizing and space planning will transform how you feel about your life and how you function in your space.

I work with your existing furniture and belongings and help you edit the items that no longer serve you. This not only maximizes space but improves functionality, increases clarity, and reduces stress. Living more intentionally can reduce impulse spending and increase focus on quality over quantity and purpose over accumulation.

Whether you need focused attention on one problematic area or a complete home transformation, my expertise in space-planning, organizing, and design will create customized systems that not only look beautiful but make room for the life you want to live.

A thoughtful, collaborative process designed around your life, your schedule, and your vision.

Step 01
Consultation

We begin by touring your space and discussing your organizing goals. I'll get a feel for your routines, stress points, and challenges, taking measurements and photos for detailed planning. We'll cover pricing, scheduling, and any questions you have.

Ask Us Anything
Do I have to be home during the organizing session?
It's completely up to you. During the sorting process you won't need to participate but some clients want to be involved or stay nearby. No decisions on edits or placements are made without your input and we work around your schedule and comfort level.
Will you make me get rid of things I want to keep?
Never. You're always in control of what stays and what goes. Our job is to ask helpful questions and offer perspective — not pressure you. We believe in keeping what you love and what serves you, and finding the right home for everything else.
How long does a typical session take?
Every project is different and your space is unique to you and your goals. Because of this, no specific time can be determined without a consultation.
Do you handle the shopping for bins and products?
Yes! We can shop for all recommended containers, bins, labels, and organizers on your behalf (product costs are additional). We measure your spaces and select items that fit perfectly, so nothing gets returned. You can also choose to do the shopping yourself from our curated product list.
What areas of Phoenix do you serve?
We serve the greater Phoenix metro area including Scottsdale, Tempe, Chandler, Gilbert, Mesa, Peoria, Glendale, and surrounding communities. Virtual consultations are available nationwide for clients outside our service area.
How do I maintain the organization after you leave?
We design every system with long-term maintainability in mind, labeling clearly, keeping things intuitive, and walking you through the logic before we leave. We also include a 30-day check-in to see how things are holding up and make any tweaks. Most clients tell us the systems are so logical they maintain themselves.
